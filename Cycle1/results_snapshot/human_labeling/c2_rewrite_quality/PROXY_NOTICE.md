# PROXY ratings -- NOT human data

The rater_*.csv files in this directory were filled by GPT (gpt-4o-mini) at temperature 0.9 with three distinct annotator personas, as an INTERIM stand-in for the human study. Every downstream number (Fleiss kappa, ICC, Spearman, per-condition means) must be reported as 'LLM-simulated proxy ratings, pending the human experiment', never as human results.
